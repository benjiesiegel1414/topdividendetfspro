The Problem I Set Out to Solve

Researching a single dividend ETF properly meant a scavenger hunt across the entire internet. One tab for the yield. Another for the expense ratio. The fund provider's site for tax documents. A broker tool for total returns. A spreadsheet to figure out price decay myself. And half the time, two sources would show two different numbers for the same fund.

Sound familiar?

  • Yields that were outdated or flat-out wrong on major finance sites
  • Tax treatment info buried in 19a-1 notices and fund documents nobody reads
  • Total return data that ignored price decay, making some high-yield funds look far better than they really performed
  • No single place to compare expense ratios, payout frequency, and inception dates side by side
  • Hours wasted every week just re-checking data that should take minutes

I kept waiting for someone to build the tool I needed. Nobody did. So in true dividend-investor fashion, I stopped waiting and built it myself: one clean platform with every critical metric for the most popular dividend ETFs: accurate, organized, and updated daily.

That's Top Dividend ETFs PRO. It's the resource I wished existed for 10 years, and now it's the exact tool I use to manage my own dividend portfolio every single day.
