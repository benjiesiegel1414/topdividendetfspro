What does each data column mean?
Symbol: Ticker symbol | Yield: Annual dividend yield | Expense Ratio: Annual fund fee | AUM: Fund size & liquidity | Total Return: Overall performance | Price Decay: Price performance since inception | Rating: Our overall score (A–F) based on multiple factors
What is the Tax Treatment Grade?
A grade (A–F) reflecting how tax-friendly we believe the ETF is for a typical taxable account. Grade A is most tax-efficient, F is least. This is our opinion only, not tax advice. Consult a tax professional.
What does the Rating mean?
Our overall score (A–F) based on yield, total return, price decay, holdings quality, fund provider reputation, and expense ratios. It's our opinion only and should be one data point in your research.
