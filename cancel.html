Cancel Your Monthly PRO Subscription

Sorry to see you go! Canceling takes a minute and there are no hoops to jump through. Pick whichever option is easier for you.

Option 1 · Do it yourself

Cancel in the Stripe customer portal

Your PRO subscription is billed through Stripe, so you can manage or cancel it yourself.

  1. Open the Stripe customer portal using the button below.
  2. Enter the same email you used at checkout. Stripe will email you a secure login link.
  3. Click the link in that email to open your billing page.
  4. Click Cancel plan, then confirm.

Send a quick email to Business@TopDividendETFs.com and we will cancel your subscription for you and reply to confirm.

Good to know

  • You keep access until your billing period ends. Canceling stops future charges; you can keep using PRO for the time you already paid for.
  • No future charges. Once canceled, you will not be billed again.
  • Lifetime members: there is nothing to cancel. Lifetime is a one-time payment and is never billed again.
  • Monthly payments are not refunded, as stated in our Terms of Service.
  • Changed your mind later? You can rejoin anytime from the pricing section.
